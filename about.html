THE PROJECT

About Image Compressor

A focused browser-based tool for compressing one image at a time and making an informed choice about its output.

What this site does

Image Compressor lets you open a JPG, PNG or WebP image in your browser, choose an output format and encode a new file using browser canvas APIs. You can compare the original and new file sizes, inspect both previews and download the output if it suits your needs. The tool does not resize images, guarantee a reduction, or replace a careful visual check.

Who maintains it

Image Compressor is created and maintained by Sourabh Ghatoye, whose professional positioning is Digital Marketing & SEO. This site does not make claims about qualifications, client results or awards.

How it is maintained

The project aims to keep its scope clear: one image-compression tool, straightforward information about its limits, and no image-processing upload service. Browser capabilities can change, so output availability and results may differ by device. Please get in touch if you encounter a problem or have a suggestion.
