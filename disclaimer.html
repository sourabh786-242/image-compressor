Last updated: November 5, 2026

No guaranteed compression result

Image Compressor re-encodes supported images using browser capabilities. Results depend on the source image, selected format, browser encoder and settings. The tool does not guarantee a particular file-size reduction, visual quality, or compatibility with every platform. A re-encoded file can be the same size or larger than the original.

Check every output

Before publishing, sharing or otherwise relying on an output, open it and verify its appearance, dimensions, format, transparency and suitability for your use. Keep the original file if it matters. The tool does not resize images. Exported files omit source metadata, including EXIF information, and browser orientation handling can vary.

Technology and availability

The tool relies on your browser's image decoding, Canvas and file-download support, along with available device memory. Supported formats and encoding results may vary. The site may be changed or temporarily unavailable and does not promise uninterrupted operation.
