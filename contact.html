Helpful details to include

If you are reporting a technical issue, mention your browser and device, the input format, and the step where the problem occurred. Please do not email private or sensitive images unless you have considered the privacy implications and want to share them.

Image Compressor does not require you to send an image to receive help. A short description of the issue is usually enough to begin troubleshooting.
