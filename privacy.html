Privacy Policy

This policy explains what happens when you visit Image Compressor and use its image tool.

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Image processing

When you select a supported image, the page reads and decodes it in your browser, then uses browser canvas features to encode the output. Where available, encoding runs in a browser worker to keep the page responsive. A limited main-thread fallback handles images up to 4 megapixels when background canvas processing is unavailable. The site does not upload image files to an image-processing server or external image-conversion API.

The browser temporarily holds the selected file, decoded image data and previews in memory. The page uses temporary object URLs to display previews and download the result; it revokes result URLs when you change settings or replace a result, and revokes image URLs when you choose another image, reset the tool or leave the page. These are temporary browser resources, not persistent site storage. Exported images are created from image pixels and do not retain the source file's EXIF or other embedded metadata.

Website requests and hosting

As with any website, your browser sends requests to the hosting provider to retrieve the site's files. The provider may process technical request information, such as an IP address, request time, browser information or requested URL, and may keep server or security logs according to its own terms and privacy practices. The site owner does not control all hosting-provider logging. No specific hosting provider is identified in this policy because deployment configuration may change.

Cookies, local storage and analytics

The current site code does not set cookies, use local storage or session storage, or include analytics scripts. It does not include advertising scripts. No third-party image-processing or analytics dependency is loaded by the current site.

Advertising plans

Advertising is not currently enabled. No advertising code, advertising cookies or advertising consent platform is installed by this site as delivered. If advertising such as Google AdSense is enabled in the future, third-party vendors may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, measure or personalise advertisements, subject to the applicable settings and requirements. The owner must update this policy and configure an appropriate consent solution before enabling advertising where required. Personalised advertising will not be described as disabled or enabled until the actual configuration is known.

Children

This tool is a general-purpose website and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. Do not send personal information by email that you do not want the site owner to receive.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the site's hosting, features or data practices change. The updated date at the top indicates the most recent policy revision. Check this page for the current explanation.
